Overview

The handoff depended on knowledge that lived with experts.

Contributors needed to export a 3D model from SketchUp, prepare and upload it in Sketchfab, then embed it correctly in WordPress. I turned that cross-tool process into self-serve onboarding and supporting documentation.

01

RESEARCH TO INSTRUCTIONS

I learned the workflow by doing it.

I interviewed stakeholders, gathered requirements, and reproduced every step myself. Testing the process exposed assumptions an expert might skip: prerequisites, export settings, where to find embed code, and how to verify that the published model worked.

Documentation became a product test: if I couldn’t complete the workflow from the instructions, the instructions weren’t finished.

03

MULTIMODAL HANDOFF

The guide and video served different moments.

The written guide supported scanning, reference, and troubleshooting. A recorded walkthrough demonstrated spatial and visual actions that were easier to understand in motion. Both followed the same sequence and terminology.

Written guideScan · revisit · troubleshoot
Video walkthroughWatch · orient · imitate

Outcome

A repeatable workflow instead of an expert-only handoff.

The onboarding and supporting documentation were associated with an approximately 40% reduction in integration time. The work also created a consistent publishing process across three tools.
