ABOUT

Research tells me where the friction is. Design gives me a way to remove it.

I’m Laticia, a UX designer with a B.S. in Cognitive Science specializing in Human–Computer Interaction. I work across research, flows, wireframes, interface content, prototyping, and product QA.

My strength is seeing the whole journey: not only whether a screen looks polished, but whether users understand the state they’re in, trust what happens next, and can recover when something goes wrong.
