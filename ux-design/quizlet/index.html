UX/UI DESIGN · INTERACTION DESIGN · USER RESEARCH

Quizlet

Integrating peer engagement features for remote learning.

Role
UX/UI Designer, UX Researcher
Duration
February—March 2022
Timeline
6 weeks
Scope
UX/UI · Interaction · Research

BACKGROUND

Improving the remote learning experience through peer collaboration.

Due to the pandemic, many schools have implemented a remote learning model. While remote learning comes with a myriad of benefits including increased flexibility and freedom, many students have struggled with lack of focus, decrease in motivation, and less opportunities for social interaction.

My team was passionate about improving the remote learning experience for students using Quizlet, a popular study tool, by integrating features that enable peer-to-peer engagement and collaboration.

01

THE PROBLEM

How might we help students efficiently and productively build their knowledge through communicating on Quizlet, so that all users are benefiting from others’ perspectives?

From user interviews, we discovered that remote learning had caused a disconnect between students, instructors, and their peers. Most students have found virtual learning very difficult and it has negatively impacted their ability to collaborate. As a result, we wanted to create a product that would facilitate learning and productive peer interaction in a remote setting.

02

REDESIGNING QUIZLET

Why Quizlet?

We chose to redesign Quizlet because we found that:

  1. 01

    It currently operates on a highly individualistic user experience, lacking collaborative features.

  2. 02

    Students already share Quizlet card sets with one another on other messaging platforms.

  3. 03

    The sudden transition to online education changed the curriculum greatly, meaning older study sets were outdated and unreliable. Study sets may also contain mistakes, conflicting answers, or be incomplete.

OUR SOLUTION BRIEF

Revamping Quizlet: Integrating Peer Engagement Features

Our solution allows users to continue using a platform they’re comfortable with, while adding features that enhance remote learning.

Forums provide a safe space for students to ask each other questions and find relevant study material. Like and comment functionality encourage additional engagement.

03

THE SOLUTION

Three connected features for collaborative learning.

01 · FORUMS

Ask, discuss, and find your way back

This new concept gives each question a clear topic and reply count, makes the next step to ask or answer obvious, and uses a visible bookmark to save a discussion. The animation is a portfolio redesign built from the original research and iteration findings.

02 · FLASHCARD REVISIONS

Improve shared study material

With the ability to suggest changes to a flashcard, users are able to improve flashcard content and help catch and fix mistakes. This ensures that information is correct and up-to-date.

03 · VERIFIED STUDY SETS

Make trustworthy content visible

Users can access expert-verified study sets, along with helpful notes to supplement learning.

04

TAKING A CLOSER LOOK · ITERATION

A/B testing with five frequent Quizlet users.

To refine our high-fidelity prototypes, we conducted A/B testing with five students who frequently used Quizlet. Participants identified areas of confusion that we had overlooked in our initial designs. Taking their valuable feedback into account, we were able to improve on various elements in our designs to create the most streamlined version.

After synthesizing the feedback, we modified the Forum page and Edit Flashcard page to alleviate areas of confusion for users. As shown below, we compare and contrast the previous designs to the new designs and describe why we made the adjustments.

01

EDIT FLASHCARD PAGE

Support recognition rather than recall.

4 out of 5 users

found the “terms and definition” toggle unnecessary and inconvenient because:

  1. It forces users to either memorize the term and the definition, or toggle back and forth to view information.
  2. It is unlikely that users would specify changes to only the term or the definition.

To increase information visibility and support recognition rather than recall, we removed the toggle and displayed both the term and definition on the screen. This eliminates the need to toggle between term and definition, minimizing cognitive and manual effort. It also enables users to provide more generalized suggestions to flashcards.

02

FORUM PAGE

Make saving recognizable and reduce visual clutter.

2 out of 5 users

were confused by the kebab menu and what it indicated.

To increase the discoverability of the “Save Forum” feature, we replaced the kebab menu with a standard bookmark icon. Users also expressed that they felt overwhelmed by the amount of information on each forum post, so we removed preview text and number of likes to reduce visual clutter and maintain clarity and simplicity.

The new animation above explores that direction further. The before-and-after image here documents the original A/B test iteration.

REFLECTION

Technology can give students greater agency over learning.

While many people associate online learning with compromised educational experiences, I believe that with the proper use of technology, students can have agency over their own learning and take responsibility for developing and communicating their ideas.

The pandemic has transformed education greatly, and I hope that I can continue to work on projects that can facilitate and enrich learning.
